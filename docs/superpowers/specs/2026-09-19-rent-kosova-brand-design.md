# Rent Kosova Brand Design

## Brand foundation

Rent Kosova is the customer-facing name of the managed marketplace for professional car-rental companies in Kosovo. The brand promise is **clear choices for the road ahead**. The personality is modern local confidence: capable, welcoming, direct, and calm. It must never imply that a request is already confirmed, that every supplier is verified before the actual verification process, or that demonstration inventory is real.

## Positioning

- Primary audience: visitors and residents who need a dependable way to compare and request an exact rental vehicle.
- Secondary audience: professional rental companies using the supplier workspace.
- Differentiator: exact vehicles, clear total prices and conditions, with supplier confirmation stated plainly.
- Descriptor: `Car rental marketplace in Kosovo`.
- English brand line: `Clear choices for the road ahead.`
- Albanian brand line: `Zgjedhje të qarta për rrugën përpara.`
- German brand line: `Klare Auswahl für die Straße vor Ihnen.`

## Verbal identity

Use `Rent Kosova` exactly, with both words in title case. Do not use `RentKosova`, `Rent Kosovo`, or the former public name `Kosova Rent-A-Car` in customer-facing copy. Use short, concrete sentences. Prefer `rental company` over `supplier` in customer copy; supplier remains acceptable in operational interfaces. Never claim guaranteed availability, instant confirmation, lowest prices, verified suppliers, or production readiness without supporting evidence.

## Visual identity

The mark is an `R` and `K` route monogram in a softly squared field. The rising route cut expresses movement without using a generic car silhouette. The primary lockup pairs the mark with the `Rent Kosova` wordmark.

### Color system

- `Road Navy` `#132A3A`: primary text and authority.
- `Kosova Blue` `#2457D6`: primary action and brand recognition.
- `Sun Gold` `#F2B544`: small highlights only.
- `Mountain Mist` `#EAF0F5`: tinted surfaces.
- `Warm Road` `#F7F8F5`: page background.
- `White` `#FFFFFF`: elevated surfaces.
- `Signal Red` `#A33A2B`: destructive states only.

All interactive color combinations must meet WCAG AA. Gold is not used for body text on light backgrounds.

### Typography and shape

Use the native stack `Avenir Next`, `Segoe UI`, Arial, sans-serif to avoid a third-party font dependency. Headings use strong negative tracking and 700 weight. Body text uses 400 to 500 weight. Controls and brand marks use a consistent 10px to 14px radius system; buttons are not pills.

## Product application

The rebrand covers the global header and footer, supplier workspace, legal/draft pages, metadata, web manifest, favicon and share image assets, package identifiers, documentation, and automated brand checks. Existing route structure, booking behavior, languages, pricing rules, accessibility, and demo disclosures remain unchanged.

## Asset set

- Primary horizontal lockup for light backgrounds.
- Reversed horizontal lockup for dark backgrounds.
- Standalone square mark.
- Browser/app icon derived from the standalone mark.
- Monochrome variants encoded through SVG `currentColor` where practical.

## Acceptance criteria

1. No customer-facing instance of `Kosova Rent-A-Car` remains.
2. Metadata and installable-app surfaces identify the platform as `Rent Kosova`.
3. The mark is visible in navigation and remains legible at mobile size.
4. English, Albanian, and German brand lines exist in the locale dictionaries.
5. The interface retains keyboard focus visibility and AA contrast.
6. Existing marketplace behavior and safety disclosures remain intact.
7. A brand guide documents strategy, assets, colors, typography, voice, and prohibited claims.
